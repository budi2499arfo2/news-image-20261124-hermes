Sum up Tuesday 24 November 2026's world news in one image: an 8-panel
grid, each panel one of the day's top stories, researched from live news
(Reuters world summary, 24 Nov 2026).

1. NATO sends an AWACS early-warning jet to Poland after three Russian
   missiles landed in the country.
2. Ukraine sinks a Russian naval corvette in a Libyan port with maritime
   drones — the war spreading beyond Europe.
3. At least 24 dead in a candy-factory fire in Indonesia, about 30 people
   still missing.
4. COP31 climate talks collapse with no fossil-fuel deal.
5. Western intelligence warns Russia could put a nuclear weapon in orbit
   within weeks.
6. Jensen Huang returns as Nvidia CEO after Trump intervened; Nvidia's
   value jumps almost $1tn.
7. Iran vows "every means" against the US naval blockade as new sanctions
   hit its shadow banking networks.
8. Carlos Alcaraz beats Alexander Zverev in Turin for his first ATP
   Finals title.

`main.py` (PIL) draws the 1024×1024 PNG to `/output/entry.png`:
header with the date, 2×4 grid of numbered panels (bold headline,
one-line summary, simple geometric icon), footer "8 stories, 1 day".
It verifies the output is PNG, ≤1024×1024 and ≤1 MiB.
